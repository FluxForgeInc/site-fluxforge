Fundamental Questions

Straight answers about how we operate.

No contractual ambiguity, no empty jargon. Technical and business clarity from the first contact.

How do you know if AI makes sense for my case?
We start every relationship with a technical and financial feasibility analysis. If a simple deterministic algorithm, a standard automation or a process redesign solves the problem at lower cost and higher reliability, that's exactly what we recommend. We only propose Artificial Intelligence when it unlocks provable operational and economic advantages.

Are my data and infrastructure ready for this?
Practically no company starts out with perfectly clean, organised data. We build our own cleaning, ingestion and enrichment pipelines. We don't require a massive migration up front: our technology integrates with any existing software, stack, database or workflow exactly as it stands.

Who ends up owning the code, the data and the models?
100% client sovereignty. We don't operate on a vendor lock-in model. All source code, configured data, fine-tuned model weights and architecture documentation belong entirely to your organisation from day one. The system can be operated autonomously by your own team.

How do you measure success and financial return?
We define metrics before writing a single line of code: absolute reduction in defect rate, minutes saved per operational cycle, reduced warehouse capital tied-up, or volume of processes resolved without intervention. Project success is audited against these concrete KPIs.

Which sectors and types of software do you serve?
We operate across multiple industrial and business sectors: manufacturing, advanced logistics, large-scale retail, insurance, financial services and energy. We are stack-agnostic: we work with public cloud infrastructure (AWS, Azure, GCP), strict on-premise environments and edge-computing devices for low latency.
