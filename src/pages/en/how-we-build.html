Engineering Principles

Four convictions that guide every line of code

We don't build on fleeting enthusiasm. We operate with industrial discipline to guarantee longevity, precision and autonomy for your business.

01

Start from the friction, not the features

We don't invent problems to use new models. We map real costs, delays and operational bottlenecks before designing architectures. If a solution doesn't remove tangible operational friction, we don't build it.

Bottleneck mapping in factory or transactional environments
02

Measure impact from day one

We define operational and financial KPIs (OEE, hours saved, stock levels, ROI) before development starts, to validate every delivery. Every software component must justify its computational cost with measurable return.

Quantitative baselines established at kick-off
03

Integrate, don't replace

We build on top of any existing ecosystem — ERPs, critical legacy systems, relational or vector databases, message queues and APIs. No rigid silos, no proprietary dependencies, and no need to rewrite software.

Native integration with any software, legacy system, database or pipeline
04

Hand over total sovereignty to the client

The code, the data, the weights and the infrastructure belong 100% to your company. We leave technical capability installed and documented so your in-house team can take the helm with full autonomy.

Zero technical lock-in or hidden royalties
